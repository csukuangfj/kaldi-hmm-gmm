Detailed Description

Decodable for an AmDiagGmm whose indices are transition-ids, with acoustic scaling.

This is the decodable used by kaldi's alignment and decoding tools (e.g. gmm-align-compiled). It maps the transition-id to its pdf via the TransitionModel (the self-loop pdf for self-loops, the forward pdf otherwise) and returns the GMM log-likelihood of that pdf, multiplied by the acoustic scale \(s\):

\[ \mathrm{LogLikelihood}(t, \mathrm{tid}) \;=\; s \cdot \log p\!\left(\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathrm{pdf}(\mathrm{tid})\right). \]

The acoustic scale weights the acoustic log-likelihoods relative to the graph scores (transition and language-model probabilities live on the graph arcs and are not included in these scores). Frames are numbered from zero but transition-ids from one.
