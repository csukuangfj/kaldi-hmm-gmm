◆ forward_pdf_class

int32_t khg::HmmTopology::HmmState::forward_pdf_class

The forward pdf-class, typically 0, 1 or 2 (the same as the HMM-state index), but may be different to enable us to hardwire sharing of state, and may be equal to kNoPdf == -1 in order to specify nonemitting states (unusual).

◆ self_loop_pdf_class

int32_t khg::HmmTopology::HmmState::self_loop_pdf_class

The self-loop pdf-class, similar to the forward pdf-class.

They will either both be kNoPdf, or neither be kNoPdf.

◆ transitions

std::vector<std::pair<int32_t, float> > khg::HmmTopology::HmmState::transitions

A list of transitions, indexed by what we call a 'transition-index'.

The first member of each pair is the index of the next HmmState, and the second is the default transition probability (before training).
