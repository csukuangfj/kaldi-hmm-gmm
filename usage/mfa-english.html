MFA alignment — English

A complete, copy-paste runnable English example. See MFA-style forced alignment for how the pipeline works and Environment setup for installing the dependencies.

Test audio

Download it with:

wget https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/asr-models/en.wav

  • file: en.wav (mono, 16-bit PCM, 24 kHz, 3.85 s)

  • transcript: Ask not what your country can do for you, ask what you can do for your country.

Step 1: download the acoustic model and the dictionary

The english_mfa model (MFA v3.1.0), the matching english_mfa dictionary, and the test audio:

mkdir -p english && cd english
wget https://github.com/MontrealCorpusTools/mfa-models/releases/download/acoustic-english_mfa-v3.1.0/english_mfa.zip
unzip english_mfa.zip        # -> english_mfa/ (final.alimdl, final.mdl, tree,
                             #    lda.mat, meta.json, phones.txt, ...)
wget https://github.com/MontrealCorpusTools/mfa-models/releases/download/dictionary-english_mfa-v3.1.0/english_mfa.dict
wget https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/asr-models/en.wav
cd ..

Step 2: run the alignment

From the repository root:

python3 tools/align_mfa.py --wav english/en.wav \
  --text "Ask not what your country can do for you, ask what you can do for your country." \
  --model-dir english/english_mfa --dict english/english_mfa.dict \
  --phones-txt english/english_mfa/phones.txt --out english/en.TextGrid

Expected output

Measured on an Apple M-series laptop (the timing line shows the audio duration, the wall-clock time of the pipeline — features + graph + alignment + TextGrid, excluding one-time initialization such as model/dictionary loading — and the real-time factor, lower is faster):

OK: english/en.TextGrid  frames=385 dur=3.85s phones=54 words=17
timing: audio duration 3.85s, elapsed 0.60s, RTF 0.156

The wav is 24 kHz while the model expects 16 kHz; the tool resamples automatically (librosa, MFA's allow_downsample semantics).

Step 3: inspect the result

english/en.TextGrid has two tiers (phones and words). Open it in Praat together with english/en.wav to inspect the alignment; the word tier contains the 17 words of the transcript:

ask  not  what  your  country  can  do  for  you  ask
what  you  can  do  for  your  country
