kaldi-hmm-gmm documentation

kaldi-hmm-gmm is a reimplementation of kaldi's HMM-GMM with Eigen: diagonal-covariance GMMs, HMM topology and transition model, decision trees / context dependency, training-graph compilation, MLE/MAP estimation, and GMM decodables for forced alignment and decoding. It ships C++ and Python APIs and does not shell out to kaldi binaries.
